Adam Fishman advises on maintaining good onboarding for the initial product experience. He emphasizes that onboarding is the critical part of the product experience that has the potential for the highest impact. He also mentions that it's the first opportunity for a company to deliver on the promise they made to customers. Fishman emphasizes the importance of aligning the brand promise with the product experience, as a mismatched expectation can lead to disappointed customers. He adds that good onboarding can either excite or disappoint customers.
